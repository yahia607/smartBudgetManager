SmartBudget Manager — README
Overview
SmartBudget Manager is an Excel workbook that records monthly income and expenses, calculates the monthly balance, compares spending with category budgets, and displays a chart and budget status.
Workbook contents
• Dashboard: total income, total expenses, monthly balance, budget status, category budget summary, and chart.
• Transactions: transaction Date, Description, Type, Category, Income, Expense, Notes, and automatically calculated Month.
• Categories: predefined categories and their monthly budgets.
Requirements
Microsoft Excel 2016, Excel 2019, Excel 2021, Microsoft 365, or a compatible spreadsheet application. File: SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx.
Getting started
1. Open SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx. 2. Go to Transactions and enter a date and description. 3. Select Income or Expense in Type. 4. For an expense, select its category and enter the amount in Expense; for income, enter the amount in Income. 5. Go to Categories to review or change the monthly budget for each category. 6. Return to Dashboard to review totals, balance, category spending, chart, and budget status.
Important notes
Enter an amount in the appropriate column only. Do not overwrite the automatically calculated Month column or formula cells. Save changes regularly and keep a backup.
